Wands

/wand: give any three words, and a wand reads them and chooses you. It's yours for good, and it explains why it picked you. There are 16 woods, 10 cores, 7 flexibilities and lengths from 9 to 14 inches, so 160 kinds of wand and 23,520 exact ones. Wands are for show; they don't change duels.

Brooms

/broom fits yours from the same three words as your wand. 100 fixed portraits (no customizing) — same idea as monster art. Each broom can be claimed by only one player; if yours is taken, you get the next-closest match. Stats are decorative: Speed, Altitude, and ridiculous ones like Squirrel Spite (all 0–10). No races. Need a wand first. /wandreset frees a claim.

Familiars

/familiar adopts one for good and lets you name it. /feed, /pet and /play each raise its friendship once a day. /scout sends it out once a day, and the more it trusts you, the more often it brings something back and the rarer it is. Now and then a rare find comes with bonus points.
