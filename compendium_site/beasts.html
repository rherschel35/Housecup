Befriending beasts

About five or six times a day, a beast wanders into #explore-velmora and says exactly what it wants. It stays for ten minutes. The first student to /approach gets 60 seconds alone to bring what it wants; after that, anyone can try. Befriend it with those things in your satchel and the items are used up.

Points

Befriending a beast for the first time earns house points, up to 5 beast points a day.

Each place has 6 common beasts, 5 uncommon (one of them is its Niffler, the thief of shiny things), 2 rare and 1 legendary. Beasts marked night only appear between 8 PM and 6 AM Central. Befriend all five Nifflers to earn the Magpie's Eye Necklace.

Study & the Beast Journal

Once you've befriended a beast, you can /study it — once a day, for any student. Each study adds a page to that beast's entry in your personal Beast Journal (field notes, habits, then the bond). Three pages finish the entry.

  • /study Study a befriended beast (once a day). Completing all three pages awards 5 house points once for that beast.
  • /journal Open your Beast Journal — overview of every entry, or one beast's full notes.

Want a private shot at a Niffler? Brew Niffler's Bait from the potions system and drink it.
