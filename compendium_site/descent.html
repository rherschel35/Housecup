What it is

The Descent is a solo dungeon beneath Velmora - one hundred floors, each guarded by a gauntlet of ten monsters. Every player has their own run: your floor, your losses, your gear are yours alone. There's no racing anyone else down, only the next monster in front of you. Every monster and boss has its own hand-drawn portrait shown right in the fight - no two floors look alike.

How a floor works

Ten monsters, one at a time

Each floor is ten monsters, fought one after another. Win all ten and you advance to the next floor.

A loss costs nothing

Lose a fight and you simply face that same monster again - a single loss doesn't cost you any floor progress.

Three losses, a lockout

Lose three times on the same floor and you're locked out of it for 24 hours. When the lockout lifts, that floor restarts at monster #1.

A stat point at monster five

Beat the fifth monster on any attempt and you earn a stat point to spend - Max HP, Attack, or Defense. A hard floor may take several tries, and that adds up: repeated attempts are real, permanent progress.

Boss floors

Every 10th floor ends in a boss - tougher than a regular monster, and worth a floor-clear bonus in House Cup points for whichever house you belong to.

Max AP grows on bosses

Your Max AP only goes up on a boss floor - clearing floor 10, 20, 30 and so on each grants +1. Regular floors don't increase it.

Some floors are meant to be a genuine wall the first time you meet them. Grinding stat points out of repeated attempts is the intended way through, not a failure state.

Combat: action points

Every fight runs on AP. Your AP and HP both refill completely at the start of each new fight - nothing carries over between monsters, so every encounter starts on equal footing. Five things you can do on your turn:

ActionCostWhat it does
✨ Cast3 APYour only source of elemental damage.
🗡️ StrikeFreeA guaranteed weak-tier hit - useful when you're out of AP.
💚 Heal3 APRestore 50% of your missing HP, but you take the monster's full attack with no mitigation that round.
🛡️ DefendFreeCut the monster's hit that round by half.
🌀 RestFreeInstantly refill your AP to max, but the monster hits 10% harder that round.

AP also regenerates naturally by +1 every round, on top of anything Rest or a free action gives you. Cast is the only action that costs real AP - everything else is free, so there's rarely a reason not to lean on Defend when you're low.

Elemental weaknesses

Every monster resists its own home element and is vulnerable to exactly one other - but you won't find that written on its profile. Casting the wrong spell gets you a normal hit, resisting it costs you damage, and finding its true weakness hits hard. The only way to learn a monster's weakness is to test spells on it and watch the fight log for resisted or super effective.

Floors aren't locked to a single element either - most floors mix in monsters of a second element alongside the zone's main one, so a strategy that only accounts for one element won't carry you through a whole floor.

Every fight plays out publicly in its channel, so anyone watching can learn a monster's pattern too - even if it isn't their turn.

Practice & replaying floors

Already cleared a floor? Replay it with /descend floor:<number> for loot — and a chance to sharpen a free stat pick. Practice fights don't advance your floor and never count toward lockouts.

  • Replayed floors always have a chance to drop that zone's material, same as a first-time clear.
  • Clearing a practice floor can grant a free Max HP / Attack / Defense pick. Odds scale with how far behind you are: 20% within 5 floors, 10% within 6–20, 5% farther back.
  • Spend any pending stat pick before you grind again — a stuck pick blocks new practice awards.
  • Boss floors can't be practiced this way — only the ten regular floors in each zone.

Commands

  • /descend Fight the next monster on your current floor, or resume a fight already in progress.
  • /descend floor:<n> Practice a floor you've already cleared — loot, and a chance to sharpen if it's nearby.
  • /descentstatus Check your floor, monster progress, stats, Max AP, and any active lockout.

The Descent only runs in its Descent channels — commands used elsewhere won't start a fight.
