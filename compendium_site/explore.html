Exploring Velmora

Five places, each with its own channel, moods, creatures and treasures, and each one remembers how you treated it.

How it worksGardenLibraryDungeonsForbidden WoodsObservatory

How exploring works

Each place lives in its own channel. Go there and use:

  • /explore See what you find: creatures, discoveries, choices. Up to 10 times a day per place.
  • /forage Search for ingredients and strange things. Up to 5 times a day per place.
  • /satchel What you're carrying. Only you can see it.
  • /use Try something from your satchel where you are.
  • /offer Leave something as an offering where you are.
  • /places Where you can go, and what's happening there.

Places remember you

Every place keeps a quiet opinion of each student, from hostile to wary, neutral, friendly and beloved. Kindness and offerings raise it; mischief lowers it. A place that loves you is more generous. A place you've upset is stingier, and it makes you work to earn it back.

Rare finds pay points

Finding something very rare or legendary earns points for your house, up to 3 exploring points a day.

Night and day

Velmora keeps Central time. After dark (8 PM to 6 AM) different creatures come out, and some things only happen by moonlight.

Secrets

Some places answer if you say the right thing in their channel. Nobody will tell you what. The Tales might.

Strange weeks

Every so often the Garden changes. While it lasts, new things can be found and new words can matter.

🚪 A Door Behind the Roses

A door has appeared behind the roses. Old wood, iron hinges, no handle. Nobody remembers it being there.

🥀 The Flowers Turn Black

Every flower in the Garden has turned black. Nobody knows why. The Garden isn't saying.

🐸 The Golden Frog

A golden frog has set up shop by the water, and it's offering trades. Bring it something. It might give you something back.
