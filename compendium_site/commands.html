Every command a student can use. Staff commands are left out.

Play

  • /duel Challenge someone. Best of three, spells chosen in secret.
  • /trio scramble Open a casual 3v3 trio duel — any houses, join either side.
  • /trio housematch Open a house-vs-house 3v3 trio duel (house role gated).
  • /grand Challenge someone to a Grand Duel (both need 50+ 1v1 wins).
  • /duelend Clear a stuck duel lock so you can fight again. Staff can /duelend @member.
  • /houseduels Each house's overall duelling win/loss record.
  • /bean Spend 1 point on a mystery bean. Might pay 5. Might be Mordy's socks.
  • /wand Give any three words and a wand chooses you. Yours for good.
  • /patronus Your patronus, cast from the same three words as your wand.
  • /animagus Your Animagus form from three words of your own — you don't pick it. Run again anytime for 1 minute of animal sounds on your messages.
  • /broom Your unique broom from the same three words — 100 fixed portraits, silly 0–10 stats fixed per broom (same on the Compendium). If that broom is taken, you get the next-closest. Purely cosmetic.
  • /broomupgrade Raise Speed or Altitude (yours or @member). Pay with Pitch Resin ×5, Descent mat ×100, or a market token.
  • /broomrace Solo 6-stage race on the Quidditch pitch, or pass opponent: to challenge on the same track.
  • /broomraceend Clear a stuck broom race — yours or anyone else's.
  • /broomnotes Permanent study notes from courses you've finished.
  • /checklist Your daily points board — what's left today across games and habits.
  • /rumor Explore secrets, student gossip, odd headmaster whispers, embarrassing ghosts.
  • /cast Banish a wild threat — pick the spell on buttons. If you stirred it up exploring, you get 25s alone first.
  • /familiar status Check in on your familiar — friendship and today's care.
  • /familiar adopt Adopt a salamander, raven, or fox for good (once).
  • /familiar name Give your familiar a name, or rename it.
  • /familiar feed Feed your familiar. Once a day.
  • /familiar pet Pet your familiar. Once a day.
  • /familiar play Play with your familiar. Once a day.
  • /familiar scout Send your familiar out to bring something back. Once a day.
  • /raid Open a 3Raid lobby — Attacker, Specialty, Tank, 10 encounters.
  • /raidstatus Your 3Raid weekly clear status.
  • /piphowdoi Ask Pip Wick how to do something (public hearthling answer from help + Compendium).
  • /gonsomethingbroke Tell Gon something's broken — staff get a jump link to your ticket.

Marketplace

Spend house points on ingredients, scrolls, titles, and the Room. Selling pays points back (daily cap).

  • /market browse The Marketplace catalogue — ingredients, scrolls, titles, Room.
  • /market buy Buy a common (3 pts) or uncommon (6 pts) ingredient.
  • /market sell Sell ingredients (3→3 pts) or Descent materials (25→3 pts). Use batches: to sell several at once (21/day cap).
  • /market scroll Buy a Hex Scroll (30 pts).
  • /market title Buy an exclusive Marketplace title (50 pts).
  • /market room Buy the Room of Requirement (500 pts). Pings Headmasters.
  • /hexscroll Cast a Hex Scroll on someone (usually 30 min, random curse — Limp Wand is always 60).

The Descent

Solo 100-floor dungeon — only in Descent channels. Practice cleared floors with /descend floor:n for loot; nearby floors can sharpen a free stat pick. Wins bind monsters into your army for castle sieges. /descend auto:True chains the next fight after each win. Idle 3 minutes with no button counts as a loss.

  • /descend Fight the next monster on your floor (or resume an open fight). Use auto:True to chain wins.
  • /descend floor:<n> Replay a floor you've already cleared, for loot (and a chance to sharpen if it's in range).
  • /descendend Forfeit an open fight (counts as a loss). Idle 3 min with no button also loses. Staff can /descendend @member.
  • /descentstatus Your floor, monster progress, stats, Max AP, and lockout.
  • /army Full army roster (paged) + sacrifice 500 → ATK/DEF — also works here in Descent channels.

Castles & Army

Army PvP in the castles channel. Bind Descent monsters, hold one of six castles, siege on Wed & Sat (Chicago). Reinforce anytime except mid-assault. One castle per player — abandon before attacking another. See the Castles page.

  • /castles Map board: owners, perks, reinforce anytime (not mid-assault), siege, abandon.
  • /army Full paged roster + sacrifice 500 → ATK/DEF (castles + Descent channels). Size (5,000) and daily bind caps (200/day) start when PvP goes live.

Potions

Brew from satchel ingredients. Some potions help in the Descent; others call private beast encounters.

  • /brew Spend two ingredients and try to brew a potion.
  • /drink Drink a brewed potion from your satchel to activate it.
  • /potions Your Potion Rep, satchel of brewed draughts, and every recipe with ingredient readiness dots.

Quidditch

  • /quidditch scramble Start a casual pickup match — any houses, either side.
  • /quidditch housematch Start a house-vs-house Quidditch match.
  • /quidditch stats Your Quidditch record and title.

Explore Velmora

Places remember how you treat them. Not everything you can do is listed here.

  • /places Where you can go, and what's happening there.
  • /explore Go somewhere and see what you find.
  • /forage Search for ingredients and strange things.
  • /satchel What you're carrying. Only you can see it.
  • /use Try something from your satchel where you are.
  • /offer Leave an offering where you are.

Beasts

A few times a day a beast wanders into the explore channel and says what it wants. First to bring it befriends it. 70 to find — including Nifflers — some only after dark.

  • /approach Befriend the beast that's here (first approach gets 60s exclusive).
  • /bestiary Every beast you've befriended - and the ones still out there.
  • /summon Call one of your beasts to show off. Just for fun.
  • /study Study a befriended beast (once a day). Three pages = 5 house points once.
  • /journal Open your Beast Journal — overview, or one beast's entry.

Your wizard & gear

Design your look in /wizard — Masculine, Feminine, or Feminine (full body). 40 gear pieces to craft or earn; earned pieces have perks while worn (never for duels or points).

  • /wizard Design how your wizard looks (including Feminine full-body).
  • /mirror Your wizard trading card (or anyone's): look, gear, title and stats.
  • /jewelbox What you own, what you're wearing, what you can craft.
  • /crafts Your craft book — recipes with ingredient dots, plus earned gear goals like Magpie's Eye (like /potions for gear).
  • /craft Make a piece from materials in your satchel.
  • /wear Put on a piece you own.
  • /remove Take off whatever's in a slot.
  • /title Choose which earned title shows under your name.
  • /cheer House Cup Bracelet: a celebration for your house.
  • /whistle Beastcaller's Whistle: call the next beast now. Once a week.
  • /secrets Keeper's Talisman: how many secrets you haven't found.
  • /nightwatch Nightwatch Pendant: night-beast heads-up on or off.

Challenges

Posted for everyone at once — first three correct answers win points. Daily every 24 hours (pings @everyone); Trial and Rite on their schedule.

  • /challengestatus What's open right now, and when the next ones come.
