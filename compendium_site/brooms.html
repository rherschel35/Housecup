The Brooms

One hundred fixed portraits. /broom claims yours from the same three words as your wand — one owner each, next-closest if taken. Purely cosmetic.

Each card is the same sheet Discord shows: a short reading of why it fits, plus that broom's fixed bragging stats. Words pick which broom finds you; the stats belong to the broom. Jump by name, e.g. #Hearthfang.
