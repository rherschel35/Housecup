The House Cup

Points are earned for your house by almost everything you do. Staff award them too, for good work, good behaviour and good sportsmanship. The house with the most points when a season ends takes the Cup. Holding castles on Sundays also pays a stipend — see Castles & Army.

Seasons

Every season has its own score, and all-time totals are kept too. When staff close a season, the winning house is recorded forever.

House Cup Champion

The top point-earner in the winning house becomes House Cup Champion. It shows on their profile for good (co-champions if they tie).

Cups won

Everyone who earned points for the winning house that season gets to count the cup as theirs, and earns the House Cup Bracelet.

  • /standings The House Cup table.
  • /points Your points (or anyone's), this season and all time.
  • /leaderboard The top earners.
  • /history Recent points activity.
  • /housecup Past seasons and their champions.
  • /profile Everything about you.

Mystery beans

/bean spends 1 of your house's points on a bean, three times a day. Most are nothing. Some pay back double, triple, even five. A few are dreadful. The odds lean very slightly against the eater, so beans are for the thrill, not for farming points. Results are public, because everyone likes a disaster.

The rumor mill

/rumor conjures a whisper from the mill — explore tips, student gossip, odd headmaster talk, embarrassing ghosts. A fresh one also turns up on its own every few hours. Believe nothing.

The Tri-Wizard Tournament

The tournament is run by the staff. Every champion is recorded on the roll, and the title stays on their profile for good. /triwizard history lists every champion so far, and /countdown status shows how long until the next big thing.
