Quidditch

Grab three players, pick a side, and take to the pitch. Five rounds, real choices every round, and a Snitch that can end it all in an instant.

What it is

Quidditch is Velmora's team sport: two sides of equal size face off over five rounds, choosing an action each round and watching the score add up. Throw together a casual pickup match with anyone, or settle it house-to-house for real House Cup points.

Two ways to play

🌀 Scramble

A casual pickup match - any houses, either side. Two players sign up for Team A, two (or four, for a 4v4) for Team B. Wins and losses only update your personal scramble record. No points, no loot - just bragging rights.

🏆 House Match

Pick two different houses to face off. Members join their own house's side. A win updates your personal house record and earns your house real House Cup points.

Both modes support 2v2 or 4v4 - pick your size when you start the match.

How a match plays out

A match is 5 rounds. Every round, each player privately picks one action, then everyone's choice is revealed and scored together:

🧹 Attack

Try to score a goal.

🛡️ Block

Cancel out an enemy attacker.

✨ Chase Snitch

Go for the game-ending catch.

Scoring

Each side's goals for the round come out to (your attackers - their blockers) × 10 points, floored at zero. Three attackers against one blocker nets two goals worth of points; three attackers against three blockers nets nothing. Blocking only cancels the other team's attackers - it doesn't score you anything on its own.

The Snitch

Every player chasing the Snitch has a 12% chance, per round, of catching it. The moment someone does, their side gets a flat +30 point bonus and the match ends immediately, whatever the score was - so a chase is always a gamble against however many goals you're giving up by not attacking or blocking that round.

If no one's caught it after 5 rounds, whoever has the higher score wins. A tied score is a draw - no wins or points recorded for either side.

After every round, the match posts a full recap - who attacked, who blocked, who chased, and the exact math behind each side's score - so everyone can see exactly why the score is what it is. That recap stays visible on the final result too.

Timing

  • Once a match request goes up, it expires after 60 seconds if both sides haven't filled up - no waiting around indefinitely for one more player.
  • Once a match is underway, each round auto-resolves after 60 seconds even if someone hasn't chosen yet, so a match can never get permanently stuck.

Points & titles

Winning a house match earns every player on the winning side +3 House Cup points for their house. You can play as many house matches as you want in a day, but only your first 3 point-earning wins each day actually pay out - after that, the win still counts toward your record, just without the points.

Commands

  • /quidditch scramble Start a casual pickup match. Choose 2v2 or 4v4.
  • /quidditch housematch Start a house-vs-house match - pick both houses and the size.
  • /quidditch stats Your scramble and house-match record, daily point count, and current title.

Quidditch only runs in its own channel in the server - commands used elsewhere won't start a match.
