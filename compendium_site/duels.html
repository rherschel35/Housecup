Dueling

/duel challenges someone. It's best of three. Each duelist opens a private cast board with the spell buttons under it (same idea as broom races), picks in secret, then both spells are revealed at once. Ties replay. There's no safe pick: every spell beats exactly two others and loses to the other two, so it's all about reading your opponent. Wands are just for show and don't change the result.

Points & records

Winning

The winner's house earns 1 point, up to 3 duel points per person per day. Duels between two members of the same house never pay points. Every duel still counts toward your record.

Streaks & bounties

Three cross-house wins in a row and the Circle hears about it. At five, a bounty goes up: whoever breaks the streak earns +2 for their house. Same-house wins never grow a streak or pay the bounty.

Rivals

Duel the same person five times and you become Rivals. Beating your rival earns an extra point.

Duelist of the Week

Most wins in a week (Monday to Sunday, Central time) earns +5 for your house and the Champion of the Circle role until someone takes it.

House Duel Night

Now and then the staff call a Duel Night, when every duel win counts double.

The Circle is watching

The Dueling Circle quietly notices the students who keep showing up to duel, and rewards them in its own time.

/duelrecord shows your rank, wins, streak, rivals and today's duel points. Only wins, ranks, streaks and titles are ever shown, never losses or win rates.

Wild Threats

Creatures slip into Velmora's channels now and then, and exploring or foraging in the Dungeons or the Forbidden Woods can draw one out. Drive it off with /cast, then tap the right spell button. If you stirred it up, you get 25 seconds alone to cast before anyone else can try — after that, it's open to everyone. Scheduled sightings and staff summons are open from the start. Everyone who helps earns points for their house. Some creatures need a pack: that many different wizards have to land the right spell before it's gone.

Attack on Velmora

When the staff start this event, a new creature appears in every threat channel every 15 seconds for a few minutes. Kills earn personal rep during the attack. When it ends, everyone's rep turns into house points at once, and whoever drove off the most earns a +5 bonus.
